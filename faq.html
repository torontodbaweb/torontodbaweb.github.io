Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help with slow database queries and performance issues?

Yes, this is exactly what we specialize in. We're quite good at identifying root causes and fixing query or server-level performance issues, assuming your hardware is appropriately sized. We can also help determine if your current hardware setup is adequate for your needs.

I'm running my database on non-server hardware. Can you still help?

Performance optimization has limits when the underlying hardware isn't suited for database workloads. However, we're still happy to engage and can often recommend hardware upgrades within your budget. Contact us for a free 15-minute consultation to discuss your options, or book our $500 Health Check, which includes a hardware review component.

What types of database performance problems do you solve best?

We excel at diagnosing and fixing query optimization issues, identifying bottlenecks in server configuration, and determining if hardware resources are properly allocated. These are concrete, solvable problems with clear root causes and solutions.

How do I know if you're the right fit for my database issues?

We're ideal for small to medium-sized businesses experiencing consistent performance problems that can be traced to specific queries, configurations, or hardware limitations. If you have mysterious, intermittent issues or enterprise-scale databases, you may need specialized support. Contact us to book a free 15-minute consultation to discuss your specific needs.

Do you offer any other services?

Of course! Feel free to contact us and we can open a conversation about your needs and how we might help! We can offer emergency help, short term help for a migration or upgrade project, or, in certain cases, offer on call support for small and medium businesses that need occasional help with database support.

Where are you located?

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Do you work with very large enterprise databases?

If your database size is measured in the tens of terabytes or your company is massive in scale, we're happy to try solving issues, but you really should have a qualified DBA on staff just to manage systems of that size. Large enterprises typically need dedicated database administrators for ongoing management.

I don't like Teams, can't you just come in in person and do things?

For locations within the Greater Toronto Area this can likely be arranged. Contact us: admin (at) torontodba (dot) ca with your details and we can discuss. For other locations in Canada, with prepaid travel expenses.

I'm in the United States and I need help but you're in Canada. How does that work?

We're able to support US businesses as well, and can bill in US dollars if that helps.

I use Postgres, Oracle, MySQL, or something else as my database provider. Can you help?

Our deep experience with Microsoft SQL Server and other products in the Microsoft ecosystem mean that you'd probably be better off in someone else's hands.

Will you be our IT department? We have a lot of printer issues.

No, we specialize in database performance and don't provide general IT support.
